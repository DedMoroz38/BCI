# Nonparametric Spectral Analysis of Multivariate Time Series

Rainer von Sachs. 2020.

Primary source: https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-statistics-031219-041138

Verified literature mechanism: Reviews multivariate spectral density, cross-spectra, smoothing and positive-definite estimation. Spectra are Hermitian matrices; real and imaginary cross-spectral components contain different information.

Transfer and limits: Motivates preserving quadrature/phase information. We use a per-window analytic-signal covariance projected to complex structure, with real SPD packing. This is not the review's wavelet/spectral-estimation pipeline.
