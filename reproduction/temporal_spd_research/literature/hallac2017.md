# Toeplitz Inverse Covariance-Based Clustering of Multivariate Time Series Data

David Hallac; Sagar Vare; Stephen Boyd; Jure Leskovec. 2017.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03161

Verified literature mechanism: TICC models multivariate subsequences with block-Toeplitz inverse covariance networks and jointly learns segmentation and clusters. Demonstrated outside neuroscience on automobile sensor data.

Transfer and limits: Motivates stationary temporal structure. Full TICC would alter the baseline, so no clustering, graphical lasso, state transition penalty or fitted cluster reference is introduced.
