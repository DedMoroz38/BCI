# Amortised Likelihood-free Inference for Expensive Time-series Simulators with Signatured Ratio Estimation

Joel Dyer; Patrick W. Cannon; Sebastian M. Schmon. 2022.

Primary source: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v151/dyer22a.html

Verified literature mechanism: Uses signatures of time-series paths in simulator-based inference and explains continuous piecewise-linear interpolation, iterated integrals and signature kernels.

Transfer and limits: Provides a non-EEG application of path-based features. Our continuous covariance quadrature and signature-covariance fusion are new per-window assemblies; the paper's ratio estimator is not implemented.
