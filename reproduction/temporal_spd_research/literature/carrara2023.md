# Classification of BCI-EEG Based on Augmented Covariance Matrix

Igor Carrara; Théodore Papadopoulo. 2023.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04508

Verified literature mechanism: Delay embedding gives an SPD augmented covariance with spatial and lagged dependence. The paper relates delay/order to autoregression and dynamical reconstruction, and studies grid search and dynamical parameter estimation.

Transfer and limits: We retain two copies and compare lag4 against previous lag1 at exactly the same dimension. We do not reproduce their classifiers, datasets or parameter search.
