# Kernels for Sequentially Ordered Data

Franz J. Király; Harald Oberhauser. 2019.

Primary source: https://jmlr.org/papers/v20/16-314.html

Verified literature mechanism: Builds positive-definite kernels for ordered sequences from signature/ordered-moment features and connects discrete sequences to continuous paths. JMLR 20(31), 1–45; verified arXiv ID 1601.08169.

Transfer and limits: Supports ordered-path features and explicit interpolation. We compute exact depth-2 time-augmented signatures locally and add their channel Gram to original covariance. Full-kernel universality does not imply universality of our truncated, packed adaptation.
