# Spatio-Temporal Covariance Descriptors for Action and Gesture Recognition

Andres Sanin; Conrad Sanderson; Mehrtash T. Harandi; Brian C. Lovell. 2013.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.6021

Verified literature mechanism: Uses spatio-temporal covariance descriptors, a weighted Riemannian projection and boosting for action/gesture recognition. Shows covariance descriptors need not be restricted to raw sensor amplitudes.

Transfer and limits: Contextual cross-domain evidence. Its projection and boosting would change the classifier; neither is copied. No direct claim that its video features are suitable EEG features.
