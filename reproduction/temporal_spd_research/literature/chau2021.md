# Intrinsic Wavelet Regression for Curves of Hermitian Positive Definite Matrices

Joris Chau; Rainer von Sachs. 2021.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.03314

Verified literature mechanism: Constructs intrinsic wavelet regression for Hermitian positive definite matrix curves, including multivariate Fourier spectral estimation with guaranteed positive definiteness. Journal version JASA 116(534), 819–832, DOI 10.1080/01621459.2019.1700129.

Transfer and limits: A literature alternative for future spectral curves. Full frequency-dependent intrinsic smoothing is excluded now because compressing it to one fixed R requires additional modeling choices. Our quadrature candidate does not implement this estimator.
